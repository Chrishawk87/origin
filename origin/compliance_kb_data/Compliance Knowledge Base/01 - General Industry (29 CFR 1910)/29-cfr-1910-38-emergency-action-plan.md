# Emergency Action Plan

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.38
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

Required whenever another OSHA standard in Part 1910 requires one (e.g., PSM, HAZWOPER, several substance standards). Best practice for any facility. May be oral if 10 or fewer employees.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Procedures for reporting a fire or other emergency
- Emergency evacuation procedures and route assignments (floor plans/maps)
- Procedures for employees who remain to operate critical operations before they evacuate
- Procedures to account for all employees after evacuation
- Rescue and medical duties for those performing them
- Name/job title of persons to contact for further information
- An employee alarm system compliant with 1910.165

## Training

Train enough employees to assist in safe/orderly evacuation; review the plan with each employee at assignment and when duties/plan change.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Keep the written plan in the workplace, available to employees.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No evacuation map or assembly point named
- No headcount/accountability procedure
- No named contacts
- Alarm system not described or not 1910.165-compliant
