# Laboratory Chemical Hygiene Plan

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.1450
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** Avetta: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to laboratories that use hazardous chemicals on a laboratory scale (the 'lab standard').

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written Chemical Hygiene Plan (CHP), reviewed/evaluated at least annually
- Designation of a Chemical Hygiene Officer
- SOPs for work with hazardous chemicals
- Criteria for control measures (fume hoods, PPE) and their maintenance
- Provisions for medical consultation/exams
- Employee information and training
- Provisions for particularly hazardous substances (select carcinogens, reproductive toxins, acute toxins)

## Training

Train lab workers on the CHP, chemical hazards, detection, and protective measures.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No Chemical Hygiene Officer named
- CHP not reviewed annually
- Fume-hood performance not addressed
