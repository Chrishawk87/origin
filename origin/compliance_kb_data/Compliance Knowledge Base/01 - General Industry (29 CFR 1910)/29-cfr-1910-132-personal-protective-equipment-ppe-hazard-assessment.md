# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) — Hazard Assessment

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.132
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** 1910.133 eye/face, 1910.135 head, 1910.136 foot, 1910.138 hand

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to all general-industry workplaces. A written certification of the hazard assessment is mandatory even though the PPE 'program' itself need not be a single document.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written certification of a workplace hazard assessment: identifies the workplace evaluated, the person certifying, the date, and identifies the document as a certification of hazard assessment (1910.132(d)(2))
- PPE selection based on the assessment for each hazard
- Requirement that PPE be provided at no cost (with narrow exceptions)
- Employee training on PPE use per 1910.132(f)
- Cleaning, maintenance, and replacement provisions

## Training

Train each employee who must use PPE: when/what PPE is necessary, how to don/doff/adjust/wear, limitations, and care. Retrain on changes.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Keep the written hazard-assessment certification; verification of training (name + date).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Missing the signed/dated written hazard-assessment certification — the #1 PPE citation
- No documented training with the required content
- Eye/face, head, foot, hand hazards not individually assessed
