# Process Safety Management of Highly Hazardous Chemicals (PSM)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.119
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1910.119(h) contractors

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to processes involving a threshold quantity of a listed highly hazardous chemical, or 10,000 lb of a flammable liquid/gas. Core for refineries, gas plants, chemical facilities.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Employee Participation — written plan of action for involving employees (1910.119(c))
- Process Safety Information — chemical, technology, and equipment data (d)
- Process Hazard Analysis — using a recognized methodology; revalidate at least every 5 years (e)
- Operating Procedures — written, for each covered process, incl. startup/normal/emergency (f)
- Training — initial and refresher on procedures and hazards (g)
- Contractors — selection, training, and oversight of contract employees (h)
- Pre-Startup Safety Review before new/modified processes (i)
- Mechanical Integrity — inspection, testing, maintenance of critical equipment (j)
- Hot Work Permit system (k)
- Management of Change — written procedure for process/equipment/procedure changes (l)
- Incident Investigation — within 48 hours; document findings and corrective actions (m)
- Emergency Planning and Response (n)
- Compliance Audits — at least every 3 years (o)
- Trade Secrets provisions (p)

## Training

Initial training on the process and refresher at least every 3 years (documented). Contractors trained on process hazards and the facility EAP.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain PHAs and updates for the life of the process; incident reports 5 years.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- PHA not revalidated within 5 years
- Operating procedures not covering emergency shutdown
- MOC used as replacement-in-kind loophole
- Contractor element (h) ignored — this is where prequalified contractors are graded
- Compliance audit older than 3 years

## Notes for the Agent

Contractors are usually asked for the elements that touch them (h), plus their own hot-work, MOC awareness, and incident-investigation programs.
