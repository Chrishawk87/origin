# Access to Employee Exposure and Medical Records

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.1020
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** As required by client scope

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies wherever employee exposure or medical records are created; governs retention and employee access to those records.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Procedures granting employees/representatives access to their exposure and medical records within 15 working days
- Retention: employee medical records for duration of employment + 30 years; exposure records 30 years
- Preservation and transfer of records; informing employees of their rights annually
- Designation of records custodian

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Records not retained for the full 30-year period
- Employees not informed of access rights
- No custodian designated
