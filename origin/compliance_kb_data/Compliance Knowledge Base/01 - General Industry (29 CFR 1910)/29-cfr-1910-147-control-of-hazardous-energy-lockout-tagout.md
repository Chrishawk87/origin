# Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.147
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to servicing/maintenance of machines where unexpected energization or release of stored energy could cause injury.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written energy control program with energy-control procedures, employee training, and periodic inspection
- Machine/equipment-specific energy control procedures (steps for shutting down, isolating, blocking, securing; placement/removal of devices; testing to verify de-energization)
- Annual periodic inspection of each energy-control procedure, documented, by an authorized employee not using the procedure
- Roles of authorized vs. affected employees
- Provisions for group lockout, shift change, and contractor coordination
- Requirements for locks/tags/devices (durable, standardized, substantial, identifiable)

## Training

Authorized, affected, and other employees trained to their role; retrain on job/procedure changes or inspection deficiencies.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Document the annual periodic inspection (procedure, date, employees, inspector).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Missing the documented ANNUAL periodic inspection — the classic F
- Only a generic policy with no machine-specific procedures
- No procedure for verifying de-energization (try-out step)
- Group and contractor LOTO not addressed
