# Bloodborne Pathogens — Exposure Control Plan

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.1030
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies where employees have reasonably anticipated occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials (incl. designated first-aid responders).

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written Exposure Control Plan, reviewed and updated at least annually
- Exposure determination (job classifications/tasks with occupational exposure) without regard to PPE
- Methods of compliance: universal precautions, engineering and work-practice controls, PPE
- Hepatitis B vaccination offered free within 10 days of assignment
- Post-exposure evaluation and follow-up procedures
- Communication of hazards (labels/signs) and training
- Sharps injury log and consideration of safer devices

## Training

Initial and annual training for employees with occupational exposure.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Medical records for duration of employment + 30 years; training records 3 years; sharps injury log.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Plan not reviewed/updated annually (missing current date)
- Exposure determination missing or done 'with PPE' assumption
- No documented HBV vaccination offer/declination
