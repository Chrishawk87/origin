# Formaldehyde

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.1048
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies where employee exposure to formaldehyde occurs at or above the action level / as defined by the standard.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written compliance program to reduce exposure to or below the PEL using engineering and work-practice controls (with an implementation schedule)
- Exposure monitoring / assessment and employee notification of results
- Establishment of regulated areas where the PEL is exceeded (where required)
- Respiratory protection program per 1910.134 when controls are insufficient
- Medical surveillance for exposed employees
- Hygiene facilities and housekeeping practices
- Hazard communication/labeling and employee training

## Training

Train exposed employees on the substance's hazards, controls, and the contents of the standard at initial assignment and periodically.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Exposure and medical records retained per 1910.1020 (generally duration of employment + 30 years).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No written compliance program with an engineering-control schedule
- Monitoring not performed to establish exposure
- Medical surveillance not offered to eligible employees
