# Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response (HAZWOPER)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.120
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1926.65

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to cleanup operations at hazardous waste sites, RCRA TSD facilities, and emergency response to hazardous-substance releases. Construction analog is 1926.65.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written safety and health program per 1910.120(b): organizational structure, comprehensive workplan, site-specific safety and health plan (HASP), safety and health training program, medical surveillance program, SOPs, and coordination with contractors
- Site-specific HASP per (b)(4)(ii): hazard analysis for each task, employee training assignments, PPE selection, medical surveillance, air monitoring, site control, decon procedures, emergency response plan, confined-space entry, spill containment
- Site control program: site map, work zones, buddy system, communications, nearest medical assistance
- Emergency Response Plan (ERP) per (l) or (q) for responders
- Written PPE program

## Training

40-hr (general site workers) / 24-hr (occasional) initial + 8-hr annual refresher; 8-hr supervisor training. Responders trained to the appropriate level (awareness through specialist).

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Medical surveillance and training records retained per 1910.1020 (duration of employment + 30 years).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- HASP not site-specific (generic boilerplate)
- No documented 40-hour/24-hour training + 8-hour refresher
- Medical surveillance not tied to exposure
- ERP missing for emergency responders (q)
