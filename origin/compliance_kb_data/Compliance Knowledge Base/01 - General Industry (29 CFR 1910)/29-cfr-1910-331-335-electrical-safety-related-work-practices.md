# Electrical Safety-Related Work Practices

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.331-.335
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: C
**Related citations:** NFPA 70E, 29 CFR 1910.269 (utilities)

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to qualified and unqualified persons working on or near exposed energized parts. NFPA 70E is the consensus method OSHA references for arc-flash.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Safe work practices for de-energized and (where justified) energized work
- Energized electrical work permit process (aligned to NFPA 70E)
- Lockout/tagout coordination for electrical isolation
- Arc-flash hazard analysis / incident-energy assessment and boundaries
- Qualified vs. unqualified person definitions and training
- PPE and insulated tool requirements by task/boundary

## Training

Train qualified persons on skills/techniques to distinguish and avoid energized parts; unqualified persons on relevant practices.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No arc-flash boundaries or PPE categories
- Energized work permitted without a permit/justification
- Qualified-person training not documented
