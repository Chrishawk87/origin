# Occupational Noise Exposure / Hearing Conservation Program

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.95
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

A written/administered Hearing Conservation Program (HCP) is required when employee noise exposure equals or exceeds an 8-hour TWA of 85 dBA (the action level).

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Noise exposure monitoring and employee notification of results
- Audiometric testing program (baseline within 6 months, annual audiograms, standard threshold shift follow-up)
- Hearing protector selection, provision, and required use; attenuation adequacy
- Employee training program (annual): effects of noise, purpose of protectors, purpose of audiometric testing
- Access to information and recordkeeping

## Training

Annual training for all employees exposed at or above the 85 dBA action level.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain noise exposure measurement records 2 years; audiometric test records for duration of the affected employee's employment.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No baseline/annual audiograms documented
- No monitoring data establishing who is in the program
- Training not conducted annually
- Standard threshold shift procedures missing
