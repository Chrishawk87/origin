# Powered Industrial Trucks (Forklift Program)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.178
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies wherever powered industrial trucks (forklifts, order pickers, etc.) are used.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Operator training and evaluation program (formal instruction, practical training, and workplace evaluation)
- Operator re-evaluation at least every 3 years
- Refresher training after an accident/near-miss, unsafe operation, condition change, or new equipment type
- Daily/pre-shift inspection procedure and removal-from-service of unsafe trucks
- Truck-type and workplace-specific operating hazards addressed

## Training

Combination of formal + practical training and an evaluation; re-evaluate every 3 years and after triggering events.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Certify operator training/evaluation (name, dates, evaluator).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Missing the 3-year operator re-evaluation
- No documented practical evaluation (classroom only)
- Daily inspection not documented
