# Fire Prevention Plan

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.39
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Required when another OSHA standard requires one (e.g., ethylene oxide, MDA, 1,3-butadiene). Best practice broadly. May be oral if 10 or fewer employees.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- List of all major fire hazards, proper handling/storage of hazardous materials, potential ignition sources and their control
- Type of fire protection equipment to control each major hazard
- Procedures to control accumulation of flammable/combustible waste
- Procedures for regular maintenance of heat-producing equipment safeguards
- Name/job title of employees responsible for maintaining fire-control equipment and for control of fuel-source hazards

## Training

Inform employees of the fire hazards of the materials/processes they are exposed to; review the plan at assignment.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Keep written plan in the workplace, available to employees.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Fire hazards not itemized for the specific facility
- No named person responsible for equipment maintenance
- Housekeeping/waste-control procedures missing
