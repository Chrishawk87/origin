# Hazard Communication (HazCom / GHS)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.1200
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1926.59 (construction)

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to every workplace where employees may be exposed to hazardous chemicals. The most commonly required program of all. Construction cross-references it at 1926.59.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written hazard communication program
- A list/inventory of the hazardous chemicals known to be present (by identity used on the SDS)
- Methods for maintaining and providing access to Safety Data Sheets (SDS)
- Container labeling system for workplace containers
- Employee information and training program
- Methods to inform employees of non-routine task hazards and hazards of chemicals in unlabeled pipes
- For multi-employer worksites, how SDSs and precautions are shared with other employers

## Training

Train at initial assignment and whenever a new hazard is introduced: hazards, protective measures, SDS/label reading, and the written program's location.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Maintain the written program, chemical inventory, and SDSs accessible to employees each shift.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Generic plan that never lists the company's own chemicals
- No named program administrator/responsible person
- SDS access method not described
- Secondary/workplace container labeling omitted
