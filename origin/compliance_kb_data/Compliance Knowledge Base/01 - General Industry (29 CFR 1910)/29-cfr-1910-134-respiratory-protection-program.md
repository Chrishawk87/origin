# Respiratory Protection Program

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.134
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

Required whenever respirators are necessary to protect employee health, or the employer requires respirator use. A written program with worksite-specific procedures is mandatory.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written program administered by a suitably trained program administrator
- Procedures for selecting respirators based on workplace hazards
- Medical evaluation of employees before fit testing/use (mandatory questionnaire or exam)
- Fit testing procedures (initial and annual) for tight-fitting respirators
- Procedures for proper use in routine and emergency/IDLH situations
- Cartridge change schedule / end-of-service-life indicators
- Cleaning, disinfecting, storage, inspection, repair, maintenance
- Air quality standards for supplied-air respirators
- Training on respiratory hazards, proper use, and limitations
- Program evaluation procedures

## Training

Annual training and annual fit testing; medical evaluation before use and when conditions change.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain medical evaluation records (per 1910.1020) and current fit-test records; retain the written program.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No named program administrator
- Medical evaluation skipped or not before fit test
- Annual fit test not documented
- No change-out schedule for chemical cartridges
- Voluntary-use employees not given Appendix D
