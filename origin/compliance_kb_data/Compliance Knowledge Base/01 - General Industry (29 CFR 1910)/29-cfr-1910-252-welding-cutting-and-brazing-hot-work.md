# Welding, Cutting, and Brazing / Hot Work

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.252
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1926.352 (construction), 1910.119(k)

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to welding, cutting, brazing, grinding, and other spark/flame-producing work. Overlaps PSM hot-work (1910.119(k)) at covered processes.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Hot-work permit system (authorization, scope, duration)
- Fire prevention/protection precautions and combustible clearance (35 ft) or shielding
- Fire watch during work and for at least 30 minutes after
- Confined-space and atmospheric considerations
- Ventilation and respiratory protection for fumes/gases
- Cylinder handling and storage requirements

## Training

Train personnel on safe operation, fire prevention, permit use, and fire-watch duties.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No fire-watch requirement (during + 30 min after)
- Permit system not described
- Combustible clearance distance omitted
