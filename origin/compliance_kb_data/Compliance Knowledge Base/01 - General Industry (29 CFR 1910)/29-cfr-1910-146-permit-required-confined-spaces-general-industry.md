# Permit-Required Confined Spaces (General Industry)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1910.146
**Category:** 01 - General Industry (29 CFR 1910)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1926.1200 (construction)

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies where the workplace contains permit-required confined spaces. Construction uses 1926 Subpart AA (1926.1200).

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written permit-space program
- Means to identify and evaluate permit spaces; signage/informing employees
- Permit system (entry permit content, duration, cancellation)
- Atmospheric testing order: oxygen, then flammable, then toxic; continuous monitoring where needed
- Roles: authorized entrants, attendants, entry supervisors and their duties
- Ventilation, isolation, and hazard control procedures
- Rescue and emergency services — non-entry retrieval preferred; verify rescue capability
- Coordination with contractors/host employer

## Training

Train entrants, attendants, and supervisors on duties, hazards, and rescue; retrain on changes or deficiencies.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain cancelled entry permits at least 1 year for program review.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Testing order not specified (O2/flammable/toxic)
- Rescue plan relies on 911 without verifying response capability
- No attendant duties defined
- Reclassification/alternate-entry procedures misused
