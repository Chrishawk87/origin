# Severity Rate (Lost Workday Rate)

**Citation:** Industry KPI (BLS/ANSI Z16.1 lineage)
**Category:** 07 - Safety Metrics & KPIs
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Measures how SERIOUS injuries are, not how often they occur: total lost/restricted days per 100 FTE. A company can have a low TRIR but a high severity rate if its few injuries are catastrophic. Not an OSHA-defined rate but widely required by owners.

## Formula

Severity Rate = ( Total lost workdays × 200,000 ) ÷ Total hours worked

## Worked Example

120 lost days over 430,000 hours → (120 × 200,000) ÷ 430,000 = 55.8 days per 100 FTE.

## Required Elements / Components

- Total number of lost (and often restricted) workdays across all cases
- Total hours worked
- 200,000 base constant

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Capping days at 180 for the rate when the client wants actual days (OSHA caps the 300-log count at 180 per case; confirm which the client wants)
- Mixing lost days and restricted days without stating which are included
