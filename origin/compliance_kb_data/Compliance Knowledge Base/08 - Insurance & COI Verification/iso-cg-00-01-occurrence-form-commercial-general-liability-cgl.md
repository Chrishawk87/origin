# Commercial General Liability (CGL)

**Citation:** ISO CG 00 01 (Occurrence Form)
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** ISO CG 00 01, CG 25 03, CG 25 04

## Applicability / Trigger

Third-party bodily injury and property damage coverage arising from the contractor's operations, products, and completed operations. The core line every hiring client requires. Written on the ISO CG 00 01 occurrence form (preferred over claims-made).

## Required Elements / Components

- Occurrence form (CG 00 01), not claims-made
- Typical required limits: $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 general aggregate / $2,000,000 products-completed operations aggregate / $1,000,000 personal & advertising injury
- Products-Completed Operations coverage maintained through the required tail period
- No exclusions that gut the coverage (e.g., action-over/employee-injury exclusion, residential, EIFS, height/exterior) where the work requires it
- Aggregate applying per project/per location where required (CG 25 03 / CG 25 04)

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Limits below the $1M/$2M contract minimum
- Claims-made form submitted where occurrence is required
- Action-over (employee injury) exclusion present on energy/industrial work
- Products-completed operations excluded or expired before the tail runs out

## Notes for the Agent

CGL is the policy the AI, P&NC, and WOS endorsements attach to. When a client says 'name us as additional insured, primary & non-contributory, with waiver of subrogation,' they mean on the CGL (and usually Auto and Umbrella too).
