# Umbrella / Excess Liability

**Citation:** Follow-Form Umbrella over CGL / Auto / EL
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

Extra limits sitting above the CGL, Auto, and Employer's Liability underlying policies. Energy and heavy-industrial owners frequently require substantial umbrella limits on top of the $1M primary.

## Required Elements / Components

- Follow-form over CGL, Commercial Auto, and Employer's Liability (schedule of underlying)
- Typical required limits: $1,000,000–$5,000,000+ (energy/petrochemical owners often require $5M–$25M total)
- Additional insured and primary/non-contributory following form to the underlying
- Drop-down / maintenance-of-underlying terms understood

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Umbrella not listing the required underlying policies (a coverage gap)
- Total limits below the owner's required tower (e.g., $5M required, only $1M shown)
- Umbrella not following form for AI/P&NC/WOS
