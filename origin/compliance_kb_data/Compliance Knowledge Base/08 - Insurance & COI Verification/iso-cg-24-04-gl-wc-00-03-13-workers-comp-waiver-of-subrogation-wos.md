# Waiver of Subrogation (WOS)

**Citation:** ISO CG 24 04 (GL) / WC 00 03 13 (Workers' Comp)
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** CG 24 04, WC 00 03 13

## Applicability / Trigger

The contractor's insurer waives its right to recover ('subrogate') from the hiring client after paying a claim. Required separately on the General Liability policy (CG 24 04) and the Workers' Compensation policy (WC 00 03 13) — they are different forms on different policies.

## Required Elements / Components

- CG 24 04 — Waiver of Transfer of Rights of Recovery, on the CGL
- WC 00 03 13 — Waiver of Our Right to Recover From Others, on the Workers' Comp policy
- Schedule naming the upstream party, or a blanket form triggered by a written waiver requirement
- Waiver also on Auto/Umbrella where the contract requires it

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- WC waiver omitted because the party assumed AI/GL waiver covers workers' comp (it does not)
- Waiver only checked on the COI, endorsement not attached
- Blanket waiver present but the contract required a scheduled (named) waiver
