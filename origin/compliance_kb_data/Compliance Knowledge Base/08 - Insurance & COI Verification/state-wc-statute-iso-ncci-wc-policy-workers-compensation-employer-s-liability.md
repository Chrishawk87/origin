# Workers' Compensation & Employer's Liability

**Citation:** State WC statute + ISO/NCCI WC policy
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** WC 00 03 13

## Applicability / Trigger

Statutory no-fault coverage for employee on-the-job injuries (Part One) plus Employer's Liability (Part Two) for injury claims that fall outside the WC statute. Mandatory in nearly every state for employers; the coverage behind the EMR.

## Required Elements / Components

- Part One — Workers' Compensation: statutory limits per the state(s) of operation
- Part Two — Employer's Liability: typical required limits $1,000,000 each accident / $1,000,000 disease-policy limit / $1,000,000 disease-each employee
- Correct states listed in Item 3.A. of the Information Page (all states where work is performed)
- Waiver of Subrogation endorsement (WC 00 03 13) where the contract requires it
- USL&H, Jones Act, or Voluntary Comp endorsements for maritime/offshore energy work where applicable

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- State of operation missing from Item 3.A. (a gap in statutory coverage)
- Employer's Liability limits below $1M/$1M/$1M
- No WC 00 03 13 waiver of subrogation when required
- Ghost/sole-proprietor policy excluding owners where the client requires owner coverage
- Missing USL&H/Jones Act on offshore or dock work
