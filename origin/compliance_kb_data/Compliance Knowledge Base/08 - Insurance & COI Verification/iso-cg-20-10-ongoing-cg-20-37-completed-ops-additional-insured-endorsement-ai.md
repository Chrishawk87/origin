# Additional Insured Endorsement (AI)

**Citation:** ISO CG 20 10 (ongoing) / CG 20 37 (completed ops)
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** CG 20 10, CG 20 37, CG 20 33 (blanket)

## Applicability / Trigger

Extends the contractor's CGL to cover the hiring client/owner as an insured for liability arising out of the contractor's work. Two forms are needed for a construction project's full lifecycle: ongoing operations AND completed operations. This is the single most-commonly-required risk-transfer endorsement.

## Required Elements / Components

- CG 20 10 — Additional Insured, Ongoing Operations (while the contractor is actively working)
- CG 20 37 — Additional Insured, Products-Completed Operations (claims after the work is finished)
- Schedule naming the client or 'as required by written contract'
- Edition date awareness: on 04/13 and later forms, AI limits are the LESSER of the contract-required or the policy limits, and coverage is tied to the named insured's negligence
- Blanket AI endorsement acceptable only if it names the party via written-contract trigger

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Only CG 20 10 provided — leaves completed-operations claims (the biggest construction losses) uncovered
- COI shows AI but no endorsement page attached
- Endorsement names the wrong legal entity or an expired policy
- CG 20 37 references only ongoing operations language (doesn't reach 'your work' in the products-completed operations hazard)

## Notes for the Agent

Require BOTH CG 20 10 and CG 20 37 in construction. 'My carrier can't issue CG 20 37' is false — all major carriers issue it, sometimes for a small fee.
