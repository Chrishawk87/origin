# Certificate of Insurance (COI) — ACORD 25

**Citation:** ACORD 25 (Certificate of Liability Insurance)
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

The industry-standard one-page proof-of-insurance form. It EVIDENCES coverage but confers no rights and does not amend the policy — the actual risk transfer lives in the policy endorsements, not the certificate. Hiring clients and prequalification platforms collect the ACORD 25 plus the underlying endorsement pages to verify a contractor meets contractual insurance requirements.

## Required Elements / Components

- Producer (insurance agent/broker) and their contact
- Named Insured — must exactly match the contracting legal entity (LLC vs. DBA vs. individual)
- Insurer(s) affording coverage with NAIC # and A.M. Best rating (clients often require A- VII or better)
- Each coverage line with policy number, effective/expiration dates, and limits (see individual coverage entries)
- Description of Operations box — names the project and the required contractual wording
- Certificate Holder — the hiring client / additional insured requesting the COI
- Attached endorsement forms (AI, P&NC, WOS, notice of cancellation) — the COI checkboxes alone are not proof

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Named Insured on the COI doesn't match the contracting entity
- COI shows 'additional insured' checked but the CG 20 10 / CG 20 37 endorsement is not attached
- Coverage expired or lapses before the project completion / completed-operations tail
- Carrier below the client's required A.M. Best rating
- Limits below contract minimums; umbrella not shown as follow-form over GL and Auto

## Notes for the Agent

Rule the agent must enforce: 'A certificate without the endorsement is a placeholder — the endorsement is the coverage.' When drafting an insurance-compliance letter, reference the specific ACORD 25 plus the named endorsement forms and edition dates, never just 'we are insured.'
