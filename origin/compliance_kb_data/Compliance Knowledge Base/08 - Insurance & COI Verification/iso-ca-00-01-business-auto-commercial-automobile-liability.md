# Commercial Automobile Liability

**Citation:** ISO CA 00 01 (Business Auto)
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** ISO CA 00 01, CA 20 48, MCS-90

## Applicability / Trigger

Bodily injury and property damage from owned, hired, and non-owned autos used in the business. Required for any contractor driving to/around a client site or hauling materials/equipment.

## Required Elements / Components

- 'Any Auto' or Owned + Hired + Non-Owned symbols covered
- Typical required limit: $1,000,000 combined single limit (CSL)
- Additional Insured endorsement (CA 20 48 designated insured) where required
- Waiver of subrogation and primary/non-contributory where required
- MCS-90 endorsement for DOT-regulated hazmat/interstate motor carriers (see FMCSA category)

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Hired & non-owned auto not covered though the contractor uses rentals/employee vehicles
- Limit below $1M CSL
- No MCS-90 where hazmat/interstate hauling requires it
- Auto not added to the umbrella's underlying schedule
