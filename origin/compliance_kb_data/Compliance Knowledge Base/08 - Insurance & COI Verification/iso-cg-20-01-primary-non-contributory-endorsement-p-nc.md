# Primary & Non-Contributory Endorsement (P&NC)

**Citation:** ISO CG 20 01
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** CG 20 01

## Applicability / Trigger

Makes the contractor's CGL pay FIRST ('primary') and stops the contractor's insurer from demanding the hiring client's own insurer share the loss ('non-contributory'). Paired with AI status so the client's own policy is never touched until the contractor's limits are exhausted.

## Required Elements / Components

- CG 20 01 (04 13 or later) attached to the CGL
- Primary and non-contributory language present in the endorsement itself, not merely checked on the COI
- Coordinated with the AI endorsement and, where required, on Auto and Umbrella

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- P&NC only referenced on the certificate, not endorsed on the policy
- AI granted without P&NC — leaves the client's carrier exposed to a contribution demand
- P&NC missing on the umbrella so the tower isn't truly primary
