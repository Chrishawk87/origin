# Notice of Cancellation Endorsement

**Citation:** Policy-specific (commonly 30-day advance notice)
**Category:** 08 - Insurance & COI Verification
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Contractually required advance warning — typically 30 days (10 days for non-payment) — before the insurer cancels or non-renews, so the hiring client isn't blindsided by a lapse. Standard ACORD 25 language now only says notice will be given 'in accordance with the policy provisions,' so an actual endorsement is needed to bind a specific notice period to the certificate holder.

## Required Elements / Components

- Endorsement providing 30 days' advance written notice of cancellation/non-renewal to the certificate holder (10 days for non-payment)
- Certificate holder named as the notice recipient
- Applies across GL, Auto, WC, and Umbrella as required by contract

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Relying on the pre-printed ACORD 'endeavor to' language, which binds nothing
- No endorsement actually naming the client as notice recipient
- Notice period shorter than the contract requires
