# RCRA Hazardous Waste Management / Contingency Plan

**Citation:** 40 CFR Parts 262 / 265
**Category:** 06 - Environmental (EPA)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to generators of hazardous waste. Large-quantity generators must maintain a written contingency plan.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Waste determination and generator category
- Written contingency plan (LQGs): emergency coordinators, arrangements with responders, evacuation, equipment list
- Container/tank management and accumulation-time limits
- Personnel training and manifest/recordkeeping

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No contingency plan for LQGs
- Accumulation limits not addressed
