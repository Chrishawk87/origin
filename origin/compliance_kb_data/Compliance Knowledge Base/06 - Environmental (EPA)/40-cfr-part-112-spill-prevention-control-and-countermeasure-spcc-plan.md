# Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan

**Citation:** 40 CFR Part 112
**Category:** 06 - Environmental (EPA)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to facilities with aboveground oil storage > 1,320 gallons (or buried > 42,000 gal) that could discharge to navigable waters. Common for oilfield and fuel-handling contractors.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written SPCC Plan certified by a Professional Engineer (or self-certified for qualified Tier I/II facilities)
- Facility diagram and oil-storage inventory
- Secondary containment and drainage controls
- Inspection, testing, and integrity procedures
- Spill response and notification procedures
- Plan review at least every 5 years

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Plan not PE-certified where required
- Secondary containment not addressed
- 5-year review overdue
