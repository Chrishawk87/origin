# Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP)

**Citation:** EPA NPDES Construction General Permit / 40 CFR 122
**Category:** 06 - Environmental (EPA)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to construction sites disturbing 1+ acre (and certain industrial facilities). Required under the NPDES construction general permit.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written SWPPP with site description and pollution-source identification
- Best Management Practices (erosion/sediment controls, good housekeeping)
- Inspection schedule and corrective-action process
- Operator/qualified-personnel designations
- Recordkeeping and permit compliance

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- BMPs not site-specific
- Inspection records missing
- No qualified inspector designated
