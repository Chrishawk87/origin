  These are the vehicle inspection and maintenance records required by 49 CFR Part 396. The DVIR
  (396.11) is completed by the driver; if a defect affecting safe operation is noted, the carrier
  must repair it and certify the repair before the vehicle is dispatched. A periodic (annual)
  inspection (396.17) is required at least every 12 months by a qualified inspector (396.19). A
  systematic maintenance program with per-vehicle records is required by 396.3. Do not alter the
  required fields; fill only the blanks. Retention: DVIRs 3 months; annual inspection report 14
  months; maintenance records 12 months (and 6 months after the vehicle leaves the fleet).
