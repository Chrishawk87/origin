# Energized Electrical Work Permit (EEWP)

> **When required:** any work on or near exposed energized conductors/parts at 50 V or more, inside the restricted approach boundary or the arc-flash boundary, where the equipment will **not** be placed in an electrically safe work condition.
> **Exempt** (no written permit, but risk assessment and PPE still required): testing, troubleshooting, and voltage measurement by a qualified person in appropriate PPE.
