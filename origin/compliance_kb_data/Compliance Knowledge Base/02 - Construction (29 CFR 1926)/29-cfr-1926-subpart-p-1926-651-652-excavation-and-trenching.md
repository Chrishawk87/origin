# Excavation and Trenching

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P (1926.651-.652)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to all excavations and trenches in construction. Protective systems required at 5 feet or deeper (or shallower if a competent person identifies a hazard).

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Protective system (sloping/benching, shoring, or shielding/trench box) selected per soil classification
- Soil classification by a competent person
- Daily competent-person inspections before entry and as conditions change (rain, vibration)
- Underground utility location before digging
- Safe means of egress within 25 ft of workers in trenches 4 ft+
- Atmospheric testing where hazardous atmospheres may exist (>4 ft)
- Protection from spoil piles, water accumulation, and adjacent structures

## Training

Train the competent person on soil classification, protective systems, and hazard recognition.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Competent-person daily inspection not documented — the enforcement hot spot
- No soil classification
- Utility locate step omitted
- Egress ladder distance not addressed
