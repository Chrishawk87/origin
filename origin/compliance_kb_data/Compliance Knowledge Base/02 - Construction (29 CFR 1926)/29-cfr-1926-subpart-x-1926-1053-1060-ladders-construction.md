# Ladders (Construction)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X (1926.1053, .1060)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to ladders and stairways used in construction.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Ladder selection, inspection, and maintenance procedures
- Proper use rules (3 points of contact, 4:1 angle, extension 3 ft above landing)
- Defective-ladder removal from service
- Training program (1926.1060) on hazards and proper use

## Training

Train each employee on ladder hazards and correct use.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Training program not documented
- Inspection/removal-from-service not addressed
