# Fall Protection (Construction)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M (1926.501-.503)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to construction work with fall exposures. General trigger height is 6 feet to a lower level (with activity-specific exceptions).

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Fall hazard assessment for each work area/task
- Selection of fall protection systems (guardrails, safety nets, personal fall arrest, positioning, warning lines, controlled access zones)
- Written Fall Protection Plan where conventional systems are infeasible (leading edge, precast, residential) — 1926.502(k)
- Rescue plan for a worker suspended in a harness (prompt rescue)
- Competent-person designation and equipment inspection
- Training program per 1926.503 with written certification

## Training

Train each exposed employee to recognize fall hazards and use systems; written certification (name, date, trainer). Retrain on changes.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No prompt-rescue plan for suspended workers — most-missed element
- 6-ft trigger not stated (or wrong trigger for the activity)
- Training certification record missing
- Written plan absent where conventional protection is infeasible
