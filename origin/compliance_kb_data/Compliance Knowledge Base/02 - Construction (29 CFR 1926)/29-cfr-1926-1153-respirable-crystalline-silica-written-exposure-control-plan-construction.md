# Respirable Crystalline Silica — Written Exposure Control Plan (Construction)

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926.1153
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: C, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1910.1053 (general industry)

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to construction tasks that generate respirable crystalline silica (cutting, grinding, drilling, crushing concrete/stone/masonry). One of the few OSHA standards that provides a sample plan.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written exposure control plan with the SEVEN required elements:
- 1) Description of tasks that involve silica exposure
- 2) Engineering controls, work practices, and respiratory protection for each task
- 3) Housekeeping measures
- 4) Procedures to restrict access to high-exposure work areas
- 5) Designation of a competent person to make frequent/regular inspections
- 6) Medical surveillance procedures (respirator required 30+ days/yr)
- 7) Employee training procedures
- Table 1 specified control methods OR objective/performance exposure assessment (PEL 50 ug/m3; action level 25)

## Training

Train employees on silica hazards, the tasks they perform, the controls, and the contents of the standard.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain air-monitoring data, objective data, and medical records per 1910.1020.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Fewer than all seven elements present
- No competent person designated for silica
- Table 1 controls claimed but not actually run in the field
- Medical-surveillance trigger not addressed
