# Scaffolding

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L (1926.451-.454)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to all scaffolds used in construction. Fall-protection trigger for scaffolds is 10 feet.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Scaffold erection/dismantling under supervision of a competent person by trained/experienced workers
- Design by a qualified person; capacity at least 4x intended load
- Fall protection at 10 feet (guardrails and/or PFAS)
- Access provisions (ladders, stair towers) — cross-braces are not acceptable access
- Competent-person inspection before each work shift and after any event affecting integrity
- Training for erectors and users

## Training

Train users to recognize scaffold hazards; train erectors/dismantlers to a higher standard. Retrain on changes.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- 10-ft scaffold trigger confused with 6-ft general trigger
- No pre-shift competent-person inspection
- Access requirements not specified
