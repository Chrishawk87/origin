# Demolition

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart T (1926.850-.860)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to demolition operations.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Engineering survey by a competent person before demolition (structural condition, hazards)
- Written survey record kept available
- Utility shut-off/verification procedures
- Hazard controls for floor openings, walls, and debris

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No documented pre-demolition engineering survey
- Utility isolation not verified
