# Confined Spaces in Construction

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart AA (1926.1200-.1213)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: C, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1910.146

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to construction work in confined spaces. Parallels 1910.146 but with construction-specific coordination duties.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written permit-required confined space program
- Competent-person evaluation to identify confined/permit spaces
- Atmospheric testing (continuous monitoring where possible) — O2, flammable, toxic order
- Entry permit system and entrant/attendant/supervisor duties
- Rescue provisions (non-entry retrieval preferred)
- Multi-employer coordination: controlling contractor as the information hub between host, entry, and other employers

## Training

Train entrants, attendants, supervisors; retrain on changes.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Multi-employer coordination duties (unique to construction) not addressed
- Continuous monitoring not specified
- Rescue capability not verified
