# Steel Erection

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart R (1926.750-.761)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to structural steel erection. Requires a site-specific erection plan and connector fall protection.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Site-specific erection plan (SSEP) where required
- Pre-planning: controlling contractor provides written notification of concrete strength and site-layout for cranes/equipment
- Fall protection for connectors and in controlled decking zones (CDZ)
- Column anchorage (4 anchor rods) and stability requirements
- Fall protection at 15 feet for ironworkers (activity-specific)

## Training

Train erectors on fall hazards, CDZ procedures, and the SSEP.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No site-specific erection plan
- Connector/CDZ fall protection not addressed
- Column stability (4 anchor bolts) omitted
