# General Safety and Health Provisions / Accident Prevention

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926.20-.21
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: Y, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to all construction work. Establishes the employer's duty to have an accident-prevention program and competent-person inspections.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Accident-prevention program providing for frequent and regular jobsite inspections by competent persons
- Designation of competent persons for each applicable hazard
- Instruction of each employee to recognize and avoid unsafe conditions and the regulations applicable to their work
- Prompt removal/guarding of hazards
- Only trained/authorized operators of equipment

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No competent person designated
- No documented regular jobsite inspections
- Employee hazard-recognition instruction not evidenced
