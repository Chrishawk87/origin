# Cranes and Derricks in Construction

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC (1926.1400-.1442)
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to power-operated equipment used in construction to hoist/lower/move loads.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Operator certification/qualification
- Qualified rigger and qualified signal person
- Assembly/disassembly procedures under a qualified A/D director
- Ground conditions and equipment set-up assessment
- Power-line safety (clearance distances / encroachment procedures)
- Frequent and periodic equipment inspections by a competent/qualified person
- Written procedure for hoisting personnel (1926.1431) when used

## Training

Certify operators; qualify riggers and signal persons; train on power-line and A/D hazards.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Operator not certified
- Rigger/signal person not qualified
- Power-line clearance procedure missing
