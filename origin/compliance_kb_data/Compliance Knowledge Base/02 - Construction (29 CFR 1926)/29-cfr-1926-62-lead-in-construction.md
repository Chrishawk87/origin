# Lead in Construction

**Citation:** 29 CFR 1926.62
**Category:** 02 - Construction (29 CFR 1926)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger



## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written compliance program to reduce exposure to/below the PEL via engineering and work-practice controls
- Exposure assessment/monitoring and employee notification
- Regulated-area and hygiene-facility provisions
- Respiratory protection per 1910.134
- Medical surveillance and medical removal protection
- Hazard communication and training

## Training

Train exposed employees on lead hazards, controls, and the standard.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Exposure/medical records per 1910.1020.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No written compliance program
- Assumed exposures not documented for trigger tasks
- Medical surveillance not offered
