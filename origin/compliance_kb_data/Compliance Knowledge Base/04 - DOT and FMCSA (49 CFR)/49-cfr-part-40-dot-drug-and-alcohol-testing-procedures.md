# DOT Drug and Alcohol Testing Procedures

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 40
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Reference
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** 49 CFR 382, 49 CFR 199 (pipeline)

## Applicability / Trigger

The government-wide procedures for DOT drug/alcohol testing that every DOT mode (FMCSA, PHMSA, etc.) references. Underlies the mode-specific programs.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Collection, testing, and MRO (Medical Review Officer) review procedures
- Specimen handling and chain of custody
- Substance-Abuse Professional (SAP) evaluation and return-to-duty process
- Confidentiality and recordkeeping requirements

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Program not built on Part 40 procedures
- No MRO/SAP designated
