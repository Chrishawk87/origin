# Inspection, Repair, and Maintenance (DVIR)

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 396
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to motor carriers. Governs systematic vehicle maintenance and driver vehicle inspection reports.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Systematic inspection, repair, and maintenance program with records
- Driver Vehicle Inspection Reports (post-trip) per 396.11 for each vehicle each day
- Roadside inspection report handling per 396.9 (signed and returned within 15 days; retain 12 months)
- Annual/periodic inspection per 396.17
- Maintenance records retained per vehicle

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Maintenance records retained 12 months (and 6 months after vehicle leaves the fleet); annual inspection 14 months.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Only driver forms, no maintenance-program records
- Roadside report 15-day return not addressed
- Annual inspection tracking missing
