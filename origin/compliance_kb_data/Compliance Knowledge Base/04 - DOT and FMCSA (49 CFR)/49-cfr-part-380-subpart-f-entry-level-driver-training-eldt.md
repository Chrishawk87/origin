# Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT)

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 380 Subpart F
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to entry-level drivers obtaining a CDL or certain endorsements.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Theory and behind-the-wheel training by a registered provider (FMCSA Training Provider Registry)
- Curriculum coverage per Appendix A/B
- Certification of completion submitted to the Registry

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Provider not on the TPR
- BTW hours not documented
