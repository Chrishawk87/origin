# Pipeline Operator Qualification (OQ)

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 192 / Part 195 Subpart
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** 49 CFR 192 Subpart N, 49 CFR 195 Subpart G

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to contractors performing covered tasks on gas (Part 192) or hazardous-liquid (Part 195) pipelines. Central to Veriforce/PEC.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written OQ program identifying covered tasks
- Qualification of individuals to perform covered tasks (knowledge, skills, abilities)
- Evaluation methods and re-qualification intervals
- Abnormal Operating Condition (AOC) recognition and response
- Records of individual qualifications and spans of control

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Covered tasks not mapped
- AOC recognition not addressed
- Qualification records incomplete

https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/pipeline/operator-qualification

## Notes for the Agent

Veriforce and PEC are built around OQ verification for pipeline/midstream work.
