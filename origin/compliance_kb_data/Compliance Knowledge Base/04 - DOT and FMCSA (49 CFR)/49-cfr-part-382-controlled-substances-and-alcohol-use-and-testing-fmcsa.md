# Controlled Substances and Alcohol Use and Testing (FMCSA)

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 382
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: Y
**Related citations:** 49 CFR Part 40

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to employers of CDL drivers operating CMVs. The DOT-specific drug & alcohol program.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written drug & alcohol policy distributed to drivers (382.601 content)
- Testing categories: pre-employment, random (current annual rates), post-accident, reasonable-suspicion, return-to-duty, follow-up
- Designated Employer Representative (DER)
- FMCSA Clearinghouse registration, queries (annual + pre-employment), and reporting
- SAP referral and return-to-duty process
- Supervisor reasonable-suspicion training (60 min drugs + 60 min alcohol)

## Training

Reasonable-suspicion training for supervisors; driver education on the policy.

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain testing records per 382.401 (varies 1-5 years by record type).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Using a non-DOT policy instead of the Part 382 program
- No Clearinghouse enrollment/queries
- Supervisor reasonable-suspicion training missing
- Random rates not current
