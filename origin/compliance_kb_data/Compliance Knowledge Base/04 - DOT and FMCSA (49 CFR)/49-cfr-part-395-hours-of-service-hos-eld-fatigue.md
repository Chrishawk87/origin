# Hours of Service (HOS) / ELD & Fatigue

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 395
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to CMV drivers. Governs driving limits, records of duty status, and ELDs.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written HOS policy: 11-hour driving limit, 14-hour window, 30-minute break, 60/70-hour limits, 10-hour off-duty
- ELD use, RODS retention, and supporting documents
- ELD malfunction procedures (8-day repair/paper logs)
- Fatigue management and driver dispatch practices

## Recordkeeping & Retention

Retain RODS and supporting documents 6 months.

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Policy doesn't match current FMCSA limits
- ELD malfunction procedure omitted
- RODS retention not addressed
