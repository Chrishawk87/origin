# Cargo / Load Securement

**Citation:** 49 CFR Part 393 Subpart I
**Category:** 04 - DOT and FMCSA (49 CFR)
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Applies to CMVs transporting cargo. Governs how loads are secured.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Working-load-limit (WLL) calculations and aggregate WLL rules
- Number and type of tiedowns by cargo length/weight
- Commodity-specific securement rules (logs, vehicles, metal coils, etc.)
- Inspection of cargo securement during transit

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Commodity-specific rules omitted
- WLL calculations not shown
