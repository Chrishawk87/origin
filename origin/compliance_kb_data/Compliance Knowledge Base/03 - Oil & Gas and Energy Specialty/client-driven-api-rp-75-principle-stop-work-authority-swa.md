# Stop Work Authority (SWA)

**Citation:** Client-driven (API RP 75 principle)
**Category:** 03 - Oil & Gas and Energy Specialty
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Required or expected by most energy operators. Empowers any worker to stop work without reprisal.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Every worker's authority and obligation to stop unsafe work
- Non-retaliation guarantee
- Stop / notify / correct / resume / follow-up process
- Documentation and trending of stop-work events

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No non-retaliation statement
- No follow-up/trending process
