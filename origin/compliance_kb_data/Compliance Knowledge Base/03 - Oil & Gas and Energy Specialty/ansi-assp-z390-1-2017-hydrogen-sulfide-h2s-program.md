# Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) Program

**Citation:** ANSI/ASSP Z390.1-2017
**Category:** 03 - Oil & Gas and Energy Specialty
**Written program required:** Yes
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: Y, Avetta: C, Veriforce: Y, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 29 CFR 1910.134, 29 CFR 1910.146, API RP 49, API RP 55, OSH Act General Duty Clause 5(a)(1)

## Applicability / Trigger

Required for any work in sour-gas / H2S environments (upstream oil & gas, refining, wastewater). No dedicated OSHA H2S standard — enforced via the General Duty Clause, 1910.146/.134, and the ANSI consensus standard.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Written H2S program: hazard recognition, physical/chemical properties, physiological effects
- Exposure monitoring (personal and area H2S detection)
- Engineering controls and ventilation
- Respiratory protection — SCBA/supplied air for IDLH; tied to 1910.134
- Contingency/emergency response plan and public-protection (wind socks, muster, evacuation)
- Rescue procedures
- ANSI Z390.1 training: initial before assignment AND annual refresher (3-4 hr, in-person, qualified instructor)
- State contingency planning where applicable (TX RRC Rule 36; NM OCD)

## Training

Initial H2S training before assignment and annual refresher per ANSI/ASSP Z390.1-2017 (3-4 hours, in-person).

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- Annual refresher not documented (most common failure)
- No contingency/public-protection plan
- Training not in-person or not to Z390.1
- Monitoring/SCBA provisions vague
