# Safety and Environmental Management System (SEMS)

**Citation:** API RP 75 / 30 CFR 250 Subpart S
**Category:** 03 - Oil & Gas and Energy Specialty
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C
**Related citations:** API RP 75, 30 CFR 250 Subpart S

## Applicability / Trigger

Required for offshore (OCS) operations under BSEE; widely adopted onshore as a management-system framework.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Safety and environmental information
- Hazards analysis
- Management of change
- Operating procedures and safe work practices
- Training
- Mechanical integrity / assurance of quality
- Pre-startup review, emergency response, incident investigation, audits, and recordkeeping
- Stop Work Authority and Ultimate Work Authority provisions

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- SWA/UWA not addressed
- Management-system elements incomplete
- No audit cadence
