# Short Service Employee (SSE) Program

**Citation:** Client-driven (ISN / operator requirement)
**Category:** 03 - Oil & Gas and Energy Specialty
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Avetta: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C

## Applicability / Trigger

Required by many oil & gas operators. Manages employees with less than ~6 months of experience or tenure.

## Required Written-Program Elements

- Definition of a short-service employee (typically <6 months)
- Ratio limits (e.g., no more than 20% SSEs per crew) and no SSE crew leaders
- High-visibility identification (hard-hat sticker/colored hat)
- Mentoring/buddy assignment to an experienced worker
- Enhanced supervision and orientation

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No SSE identification method
- No mentoring assignment
- Ratio limits omitted
