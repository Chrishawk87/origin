# Simultaneous Operations (SIMOPS)

**Citation:** OSHA PSM 29 CFR 1910.119 (Hot Work/MOC/PHA)
**Category:** 03 - Oil & Gas and Energy Specialty
**Written program required:** Conditional
**Agencies commonly requiring (Y=commonly, C=client/scope-driven):** ISN: C, Veriforce: C, PEC: C
**Related citations:** 1910.119(k) hot work, 1910.119(l) MOC, API RP 75

## Applicability / Trigger

Required at sites where two or more activities occur concurrently in the same/adjacent areas (drilling + production, hot work near live process, crane lifts over process, marine + production).

## Required Written-Program Elements

- SIMOPS matrix identifying concurrent-activity conflicts and their combined hazards
- Permit-to-work cross-referencing between concurrent permits
- Designated authority (e.g., person-in-charge/OIM) to authorize or stop concurrent operations
- Communication and daily SIMOPS coordination meetings
- Integration with Management of Change and Process Hazard Analysis

## Common Failure Points (why reviewers reject it)

- No SIMOPS matrix
- No single authority to halt conflicting operations
- Permits not cross-referenced
