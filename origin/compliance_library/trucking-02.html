1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to manage driver hours of service (HOS) and control fatigue so that no driver operates a commercial motor vehicle while ill, fatigued, or in excess of allowable driving limits. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 49 CFR Part 395.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} drivers subject to the hours-of-service rules and to the dispatch and management personnel who assign their work. All drivers shall record their duty status and observe the driving and on-duty limits.

3.0 Definitions

Hours of Service (HOS) — the maximum on-duty, driving, and rest periods permitted under 49 CFR Part 395.

Electronic Logging Device (ELD) — a device that automatically records driving time and duty status per 49 CFR 395.8 and Subpart B.

On-Duty Time — all time from when a driver begins work until relieved, as defined in 49 CFR 395.2.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Safety Manager: shall monitor logs for compliance, investigate violations, and ensure ELDs are registered and functioning.

▪ Dispatchers: shall plan trips within legal hours and shall not require or permit a driver to exceed HOS limits or drive while fatigued.

▪ Drivers: shall accurately record duty status, take required rest, and decline to drive when fatigued, ill, or out of hours.

5.0 Program Requirements

Driving & On-Duty Limits

▪ Drivers shall observe the applicable driving-time, on-duty, and rest-break limits established in 49 CFR 395.3 (property-carrying) or 395.5 (passenger-carrying).

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall not schedule or dispatch a driver in a manner that would require a violation of the hours-of-service rules.

Records of Duty Status / ELD

▪ Drivers shall record their duty status using a compliant electronic logging device unless exempt under 49 CFR 395.8(a).

▪ Supporting documents and logs shall be retained for at least six months and made available for inspection.

▪ Log falsification is prohibited; drivers and the carrier shall ensure records are true and correct.

Fatigue Management

▪ No driver shall operate a commercial motor vehicle while ability or alertness is so impaired by fatigue or illness as to make operation unsafe, per 49 CFR 392.3.

▪ Drivers shall report fatigue and shall be relieved from duty when continued operation would be unsafe.

6.0 Training

▪ Drivers shall be trained on the hours-of-service rules, ELD use, and fatigue recognition at hire and periodically thereafter.

▪ Dispatch and management shall be trained on lawful trip planning within HOS limits.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Records of duty status and supporting documents shall be retained per 49 CFR 395.8(k).

▪ ELD malfunction records and reassigned-driving records shall be maintained as required.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 49 CFR Part 395 — Hours of Service of Drivers

▪ 49 CFR 392.3 — Ill or Fatigued Operator
