1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees from inhalation of harmful atmospheres through the proper selection and use of respirators. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1910.134.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees who are required to wear respirators, and to voluntary respirator users, during operations where airborne contaminants or oxygen-deficient atmospheres may be present.

3.0 Definitions

Respirator — a device that protects the wearer from inhaling hazardous atmospheres.

Fit Test — a protocol to evaluate the seal of a tight-fitting respirator on an individual.

Assigned Protection Factor (APF) — the level of protection a respirator class is expected to provide.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall administer the program, evaluate its effectiveness, and ensure medical evaluations and fit testing are completed.

▪ Supervisors: shall ensure only medically cleared, fit-tested, and trained employees use respirators.

▪ Employees: shall use respirators as trained, perform seal checks, and maintain and store equipment properly.

5.0 Program Requirements

Written Program & Medical Evaluation

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall maintain a written program administered by a qualified program administrator.

▪ Employees shall receive a medical evaluation to determine fitness before fit testing and use.

Fit Testing & Selection

▪ Employees required to wear tight-fitting respirators shall be fit tested before initial use and at least annually thereafter.

▪ Respirators shall be selected based on the hazard, the assigned protection factor, and NIOSH certification.

Use, Maintenance & Cartridge Change

▪ Employees shall perform user seal checks each time a tight-fitting respirator is donned.

▪ A cartridge change-out schedule shall be established for air-purifying respirators used against gases and vapors.

▪ Respirators shall be cleaned, inspected, and stored to prevent damage and contamination.

Voluntary Use

▪ Employees who voluntarily use respirators (other than filtering facepieces) shall be provided the information in Appendix D and shall meet applicable medical and cleaning requirements.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained before use and annually on respirator operation, limitations, donning/doffing, seal checks, and maintenance.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Medical evaluations, fit-test results, and training records shall be maintained.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910.134 — Respiratory Protection
