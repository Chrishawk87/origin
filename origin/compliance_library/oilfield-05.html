1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees from fall hazards. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1910 Subpart D (walking-working surfaces) for general-industry work and 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M for construction work.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees exposed to fall hazards, including work on elevated platforms, tanks, rigs, structures, and near unprotected edges or openings.

3.0 Definitions

Personal Fall Arrest System (PFAS) — an anchorage, full-body harness, and connector used to arrest a fall.

Anchorage — a secure point of attachment for fall-protection equipment.

Qualified / Competent Person — an individual capable of identifying hazards and authorized to take corrective action.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator / Competent Person: shall identify fall hazards, select protection systems, and inspect the program.

▪ Supervisors: shall ensure fall-protection systems are in place and used before work at height begins.

▪ Employees: shall use fall protection as required, inspect equipment before use, and maintain 100% tie-off.

5.0 Program Requirements

Trigger Heights & Systems

▪ Fall protection shall be provided at 4 feet in general industry and at 6 feet in construction, and at any height over dangerous equipment.

▪ Acceptable systems include guardrails, personal fall arrest systems, safety nets, or positioning systems appropriate to the task.

Equipment & Anchorage

▪ Personal fall arrest systems shall use a full-body harness and shall limit arresting forces in accordance with the applicable standard.

▪ Anchorages for personal fall arrest shall be capable of supporting at least 5,000 pounds per employee, or be designed by a qualified person with a safety factor of at least two.

▪ Equipment shall be inspected before each use and removed from service if defective or after arresting a fall.

100% Tie-Off & Rescue

▪ Employees exposed to fall hazards shall maintain 100% tie-off when using personal fall arrest.

▪ A prompt rescue plan shall be in place for any employee who may fall while using a PFAS.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained by a competent person on fall hazards, system use, inspection, and rescue prior to exposure.

▪ Retraining shall occur when conditions change or when an employee shows inadequate understanding.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Equipment inspection, training, and rescue-plan records shall be maintained.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910 Subpart D — Walking-Working Surfaces; 29 CFR 1910.140 — Personal Fall Protection Systems

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M — Fall Protection (1926.501 / .502)
