1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to control hazardous energy during the servicing and maintenance of machines and equipment, preventing unexpected energization, start-up, or release of stored energy. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1910.147.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees who service or maintain equipment where the unexpected release of energy could cause injury, and to all energy sources including electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, chemical, thermal, and gravitational.

3.0 Definitions

Authorized Employee — a person who locks or tags out equipment to perform servicing or maintenance.

Affected Employee — a person whose job requires operating equipment being serviced or working in the area.

Energy Isolating Device — a mechanical device that physically prevents the transmission or release of energy.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall develop energy-control procedures, provide locks and tags, and conduct the annual periodic inspection.

▪ Authorized Employees: shall apply and remove their own locks/tags and verify zero energy before work begins.

▪ Affected Employees: shall not attempt to start or energize equipment that is locked or tagged out.

5.0 Program Requirements

Energy-Control Procedures

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall develop and document specific energy-control procedures for equipment serviced, including steps for shutdown, isolation, blocking, and securing.

▪ Locks and tags shall be standardized, durable, and used only for energy control.

Application & Verification

▪ Authorized employees shall notify affected employees before applying lockout/tagout.

▪ All energy sources shall be isolated, and stored energy shall be relieved, disconnected, or restrained.

▪ Authorized employees shall verify a zero-energy state before beginning work.

Group, Shift Change & Removal

▪ Group lockout procedures shall protect each employee with a personal lock or equivalent.

▪ Procedures shall address orderly transfer of lockout/tagout during shift or personnel changes.

▪ Only the employee who applied a lock/tag shall remove it, except under the documented exception procedure.

Periodic Inspection

▪ A periodic inspection of energy-control procedures shall be conducted at least annually and documented.

6.0 Training

▪ Authorized and affected employees shall be trained on the program, the hazards, and the energy-control procedures relevant to their role.

▪ Retraining shall occur when job assignments, equipment, or procedures change, or when inspection reveals deficiencies.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Written energy-control procedures, annual periodic-inspection certifications, and training records shall be maintained.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910.147 — The Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout)
