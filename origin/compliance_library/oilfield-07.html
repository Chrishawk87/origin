1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees from exposure to hydrogen sulfide (H2S), a toxic and flammable gas common in oil and gas operations. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with applicable OSHA exposure limits, host-operator requirements, and the ANSI/ASSP Z390.1 accepted practices for H2S training.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees who may be exposed to H2S at wellsites, tank batteries, gas plants, and related facilities, and to all operations where H2S may be present.

3.0 Definitions

Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) — a colorless, flammable, highly toxic gas with a rotten-egg odor at low concentrations that deadens the sense of smell at higher concentrations.

Action Level / Alarm Set Point — the concentration at which detection alarms and protective actions are initiated, commonly 10 ppm (low) and 15–20 ppm (high) or as set by the operator.

SCBA / Supplied-Air — atmosphere-supplying respirators required for entry into or escape from IDLH H2S atmospheres.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall assess H2S exposure potential, establish monitoring and alarm set points, and maintain detection and respiratory equipment.

▪ Supervisors: shall conduct site-specific H2S briefings, verify monitors are functional, and enforce the contingency plan.

▪ Employees: shall wear personal H2S monitors where required, respond to alarms, and follow escape and muster procedures.

5.0 Program Requirements

Exposure Assessment & Monitoring

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall assess each worksite for H2S potential and shall comply with applicable OSHA permissible exposure limits and host-operator limits.

▪ Personal and/or area H2S monitors with audible and visual alarms shall be used where H2S may be present; monitors shall be calibrated and bump-tested per the manufacturer's schedule.

Contingency & Escape

▪ A site-specific H2S contingency plan shall be established, including wind-direction indicators, briefing/muster areas, evacuation routes, and buddy systems.

▪ Escape respiratory protection (escape SCBA or equivalent) shall be available where concentrations could reach IDLH levels; supplied-air or SCBA shall be used for entry or rescue in such atmospheres.

Site Briefing

▪ A pre-job H2S safety briefing shall be conducted at sites where H2S is or may be present, covering alarm set points, muster points, and wind direction.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall complete H2S awareness training consistent with ANSI/ASSP Z390.1 before assignment to areas where H2S may be present, and refresher training as required by the operator (commonly annually).

▪ Training shall cover H2S properties, health effects, detection, respiratory protection, and rescue and first-aid procedures.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Training certifications, monitor calibration/bump-test logs, and site contingency plans shall be maintained.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910.1000 — Air Contaminants (applicable PELs); OSHA General Duty Clause, Section 5(a)(1)

▪ ANSI/ASSP Z390.1 — Accepted Practices for H2S Training Programs

▪ API RP 49 and API RP 76 — Recommended practices for drilling/servicing and contractor safety in H2S environments
