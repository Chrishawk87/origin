1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees during crane, hoist, and rigging operations in construction. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC and the rigging requirements of Subpart H.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} operations involving cranes, derricks, hoists, and rigging used in construction, and to operators, riggers, signal persons, and affected employees.

3.0 Definitions

Qualified Rigger — a rigger who meets the criteria for a qualified person and can solve rigging problems, per 29 CFR 1926.1401.

Qualified Signal Person — a person who meets the qualification requirements of 29 CFR 1926.1428.

Certified Operator — a crane operator certified/qualified per 29 CFR 1926.1427.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Crane Operator: shall be certified/qualified, conduct inspections, and operate within the load chart and manufacturer limits.

▪ Qualified Rigger: shall select, inspect, and attach rigging for hoisting operations, including assembly/disassembly and work near the load.

▪ Qualified Signal Person: shall direct crane movements where required by 29 CFR 1926.1419.

▪ Supervisors: shall ensure ground conditions, clearances, and lift plans are adequate before operations.

5.0 Program Requirements

Operator & Personnel Qualification

▪ Crane operators shall be certified or qualified in accordance with 29 CFR 1926.1427.

▪ Riggers and signal persons shall be qualified in accordance with 29 CFR 1926.1401 and 1926.1428.

Inspection & Assembly

▪ Cranes shall receive shift, monthly, and annual inspections in accordance with 29 CFR 1926.1412.

▪ Assembly and disassembly shall be conducted under the supervision of a qualified/competent person per 29 CFR 1926.1403–1406.

▪ Rigging equipment shall be inspected before use and shall not be loaded beyond its recommended safe working load, per 29 CFR 1926.251.

Power Lines & Operations

▪ The minimum clearances from energized power lines required by 29 CFR 1926.1407–1411 shall be maintained.

▪ The work area shall be barricaded to keep employees out of the swing radius, and no employee shall be under a suspended load.

6.0 Training

▪ Operators, riggers, signal persons, and affected employees shall be trained for their roles per Subpart CC.

▪ Training shall address load charts, hazards, power-line clearances, and signaling.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Operator certifications, crane inspection records, and rigging inspection records shall be maintained and available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC — Cranes and Derricks in Construction

▪ 29 CFR 1926.251 — Rigging Equipment for Material Handling
