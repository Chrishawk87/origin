1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to identify, mentor, and manage short service employees (SSEs) in order to reduce the elevated incident risk associated with new or less-experienced workers. This program is maintained to meet host-operator and contractor-management (ISNetworld / PEC / Avetta) requirements.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees classified as short service (generally those with less than six months of experience with the company or in their current position), and to the supervisors and mentors responsible for them.

3.0 Definitions

Short Service Employee (SSE) — an employee with less than six months of continuous service or experience in their current position or with {{COMPANY NAME}}, unless a different period is specified by the host operator.

Mentor — an experienced employee assigned to guide and monitor an SSE.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Management: shall establish SSE identification, ratio limits, and mentoring requirements consistent with operator expectations.

▪ Supervisors: shall identify SSEs, assign mentors, limit SSE work assignments, and track SSE status.

▪ Mentors: shall guide, observe, and coach assigned SSEs and shall intervene when unsafe conditions arise.

▪ Short Service Employees: shall wear the required identifier, work under mentorship, and ask questions before performing unfamiliar tasks.

5.0 Program Requirements

Identification

▪ SSEs shall be visually identified (e.g., a distinctive hard-hat sticker, colored hard hat, or vest) so they are recognizable on site.

Crew Ratios & Restrictions

▪ Crew composition shall limit the number of SSEs; as a default, no crew shall exceed 20% SSEs, and no more than one SSE shall be assigned per experienced mentor, unless the host operator specifies otherwise.

▪ SSEs shall not be assigned to perform high-risk or critical tasks without direct supervision by a qualified experienced employee.

Mentoring & Tracking

▪ Each SSE shall be assigned an experienced mentor for the duration of the short-service period.

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall maintain a log of SSE status, start dates, mentors, and graduation dates.

6.0 Training

▪ SSEs shall receive orientation and job-specific training before beginning work, and shall be evaluated before removal of short-service status.

▪ Supervisors and mentors shall be briefed on their responsibilities under this program.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ The SSE tracking log and associated orientation and evaluation records shall be maintained and available to host operators on request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ Industry accepted practice (e.g., IADC / operator SSE guidelines). No single OSHA standard governs SSE programs; requirements are set by host operators and contractor-management platforms.
