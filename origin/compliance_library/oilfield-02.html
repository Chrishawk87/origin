1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} for assessing workplace hazards and selecting, providing, and using personal protective equipment (PPE) to protect employees from injury. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1910 Subpart I.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees and operations where hazards that require PPE are present or may be present. All personnel shall wear required PPE as directed by this program, job requirements, and the host operator.

3.0 Definitions

PPE — equipment worn to minimize exposure to hazards that cause injury or illness, including eye, face, head, foot, hand, and body protection.

Hazard Assessment — a documented evaluation of a work area or task to identify hazards requiring PPE.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall conduct and certify written hazard assessments and ensure appropriate PPE is available.

▪ Supervisors: shall enforce PPE use, verify condition, and ensure employees are trained prior to assignment.

▪ Employees: shall properly wear, inspect, maintain, and store assigned PPE and report defective equipment.

5.0 Program Requirements

Hazard Assessment & Selection

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall assess each work area and task for hazards and shall document a written certification of that assessment as required by 29 CFR 1910.132(d).

▪ PPE shall be selected to protect against the identified hazards and shall properly fit each affected employee.

Provision of PPE

▪ Required PPE shall be provided at no cost to employees, except for items specifically exempted by 29 CFR 1910.132(h) (e.g., non-specialty safety-toe footwear and prescription safety eyewear where permitted).

Specific PPE Requirements

▪ Eye and face protection meeting ANSI Z87.1 shall be worn where a hazard of flying particles, chemicals, or radiation exists (29 CFR 1910.133).

▪ Head protection (hard hats) meeting ANSI Z89.1 shall be worn where there is a risk of head injury (29 CFR 1910.135).

▪ Foot protection meeting ASTM standards shall be worn where foot hazards exist (29 CFR 1910.136).

▪ Hand protection shall be worn where hands are exposed to cuts, chemicals, burns, or absorption hazards (29 CFR 1910.138).

▪ Flame-resistant clothing (FRC) shall be worn where required by the host operator or where a flash-fire hazard exists.

Inspection & Maintenance

▪ PPE shall be inspected before each use; defective or damaged PPE shall be removed from service immediately.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained before being assigned work requiring PPE, and shall demonstrate an understanding of when PPE is necessary, what PPE is required, and how to don, doff, adjust, and maintain it.

▪ Retraining shall occur when workplace changes render prior training obsolete or when an employee demonstrates inadequate understanding.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ The written hazard-assessment certification and PPE training records shall be maintained and available for review.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910.132 — General Requirements (Subpart I)

▪ 29 CFR 1910.133 / .135 / .136 / .138 — Eye/Face, Head, Foot, Hand Protection

▪ ANSI Z87.1, ANSI Z89.1, ASTM F2413 — PPE consensus standards
