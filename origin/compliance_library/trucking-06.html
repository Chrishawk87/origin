1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to report, record, and investigate every vehicle accident so that causes are identified and recurrences prevented. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall maintain the accident register required by 49 CFR 390.15 and shall report as required by regulation and host operators.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} drivers and vehicles and to any accident involving a company-operated commercial motor vehicle, regardless of fault.

3.0 Definitions

DOT-Recordable Accident — an accident involving a fatality, injury requiring treatment away from the scene, or disabling vehicle damage requiring tow-away, per 49 CFR 390.5.

Accident Register — the record of accidents a motor carrier must maintain under 49 CFR 390.15.

Post-Accident Testing — controlled-substances and alcohol testing triggered by qualifying accidents under 49 CFR 382.303.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Safety Manager: shall maintain the accident register, coordinate investigations, determine preventability, and initiate post-accident testing when required.

▪ Drivers: shall report every accident immediately, secure the scene, render aid, notify authorities, and cooperate with the investigation and any required testing.

▪ Supervisors: shall respond, gather facts and documentation, and support corrective action.

5.0 Program Requirements

Immediate Response & Notification

▪ Drivers shall stop, provide aid, notify emergency services and law enforcement as required, and notify {{COMPANY NAME}} as soon as it is safe to do so.

▪ Drivers shall not admit fault and shall document the scene (photos, witnesses, conditions) where safe.

Post-Accident Testing

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall conduct post-accident controlled-substances and alcohol testing whenever the criteria of 49 CFR 382.303 are met.

▪ Testing shall follow the timing requirements and the DOT procedures of 49 CFR Part 40.

Recording & Investigation

▪ Every DOT-recordable accident shall be entered in the accident register maintained under 49 CFR 390.15.

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall investigate each accident to determine root cause and preventability and shall implement corrective action.

6.0 Training

▪ Drivers shall be trained on accident-scene procedures, notification requirements, and their post-accident testing obligations.

▪ Supervisors shall be trained on investigation and preventability determination.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ The accident register shall be retained for three years after each accident, per 49 CFR 390.15(b).

▪ Investigation reports, testing records, and corrective actions shall be maintained and made available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 49 CFR 390.15 — Assistance in Investigations and Accident Register

▪ 49 CFR 382.303 — Post-Accident Testing
