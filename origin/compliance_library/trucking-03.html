1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to systematically inspect, repair, and maintain every commercial motor vehicle so that it is in safe operating condition at all times. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 49 CFR Part 396, including the Driver Vehicle Inspection Report (DVIR) requirements.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all commercial motor vehicles operated by {{COMPANY NAME}}, including tractors, trailers, and intermodal equipment, and to the drivers and maintenance personnel responsible for them.

3.0 Definitions

DVIR — Driver Vehicle Inspection Report required by 49 CFR 396.11 and 396.13.

Out-of-Service Condition — a defect or condition that renders a vehicle unsafe to operate, per the CVSA out-of-service criteria and Part 396.

Systematic Inspection — the scheduled periodic inspection and maintenance program required by 49 CFR 396.3.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Maintenance Manager: shall establish and document a systematic inspection and maintenance schedule and retain maintenance records per 49 CFR 396.3.

▪ Drivers: shall perform pre-trip inspections, prepare a DVIR at the end of each driving day, and shall not operate a vehicle with an out-of-service defect.

▪ Mechanics / Qualified Inspectors: shall be qualified per 49 CFR 396.19 and shall certify repairs and annual inspections.

5.0 Program Requirements

Driver Inspections & DVIR

▪ Drivers shall be satisfied the vehicle is in safe operating condition before operating it, per 49 CFR 396.13.

▪ Drivers shall prepare a written post-trip DVIR when a defect or deficiency is discovered or reported, per 49 CFR 396.11.

▪ Defects affecting safe operation shall be corrected and certified before the vehicle is dispatched.

Systematic Maintenance

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall systematically inspect, repair, and maintain all vehicles under its control and keep the required maintenance records, per 49 CFR 396.3.

▪ Each vehicle shall pass a periodic (annual) inspection meeting the standards of 49 CFR 396.17 and Appendix A to Part 396.

Out-of-Service Vehicles

▪ Any vehicle declared out of service shall not be operated until the defect is corrected, per 49 CFR 396.9.

▪ Out-of-service repairs shall be documented and certified by a qualified person.

6.0 Training

▪ Drivers shall be trained to perform pre-trip and post-trip inspections and to complete the DVIR accurately.

▪ Inspectors and mechanics shall meet the qualification and training requirements of 49 CFR 396.19.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Maintenance and inspection records shall be retained per 49 CFR 396.3(c); annual inspection documentation per 396.17; DVIRs per 396.11.

▪ Records shall be made available to auditors and host operators upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 49 CFR Part 396 — Inspection, Repair, and Maintenance

▪ 49 CFR 396.11 & 396.13 — Driver Vehicle Inspection Reports

▪ 49 CFR 396.17 — Periodic Inspection
