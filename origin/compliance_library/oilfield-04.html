1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees from the hazards of entry into permit-required confined spaces (PRCS). {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1910.146 (general industry) and, where construction work is performed, 29 CFR 1926 Subpart AA.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees who may enter, attend, or supervise entry into confined spaces such as tanks, vessels, cellars, excavations, and vaults where atmospheric or engulfment hazards may exist.

3.0 Definitions

Confined Space — a space large enough to enter, with limited means of entry/exit, not designed for continuous occupancy.

Permit-Required Confined Space (PRCS) — a confined space with a hazardous atmosphere, engulfment potential, entrapment configuration, or other serious hazard.

Entry Supervisor / Attendant / Authorized Entrant — the roles required to conduct a permitted entry safely.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall identify PRCS, maintain the permit system, and ensure atmospheric-testing and rescue provisions are in place.

▪ Entry Supervisor: shall authorize entry, verify conditions and permits, and terminate entry when required.

▪ Attendant: shall monitor entrants continuously and initiate rescue procedures when necessary.

▪ Authorized Entrants: shall follow entry procedures, use required equipment, and exit immediately on any warning.

5.0 Program Requirements

Identification & Evaluation

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall identify and evaluate all confined spaces at each worksite and classify permit-required spaces.

▪ Where the host operator has classified spaces, {{COMPANY NAME}} shall coordinate and comply with those classifications.

Permit System & Testing

▪ A written entry permit shall be completed and posted before each permitted entry.

▪ The atmosphere shall be tested for oxygen, flammability, and toxicity (including H2S where present) before and continuously during entry.

▪ Ventilation shall be provided as needed to maintain a safe atmosphere.

Attendant & Rescue

▪ An attendant shall be stationed outside the space during all entries.

▪ Rescue provisions, including non-entry retrieval where feasible, shall be arranged before entry; rescue services shall be capable of timely response.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained in their assigned roles (entrant, attendant, entry supervisor) prior to entry duties.

▪ Rescue personnel shall be trained and drilled at least annually.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Completed entry permits shall be retained for at least one year and reviewed to revise the program as needed.

▪ Training and rescue-drill records shall be maintained.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910.146 — Permit-Required Confined Spaces

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart AA — Confined Spaces in Construction
