1.0 Purpose

This Hazard Communication Program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to ensure the hazards of all chemicals on construction worksites are evaluated and communicated to employees. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1926.59, which incorporates the OSHA Hazard Communication Standard, 29 CFR 1910.1200, and the Globally Harmonized System (GHS).

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees and multi-employer worksite partners exposed to hazardous chemicals such as adhesives, solvents, fuels, concrete products, coatings, and silica-generating materials during construction.

3.0 Definitions

Hazardous Chemical — any chemical classified as a physical or health hazard under 29 CFR 1910.1200.

Safety Data Sheet (SDS) — a standardized 16-section document providing information on a chemical's hazards and safe handling.

GHS — the Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall maintain the written program, chemical inventory, and SDS library, and ensure labels and training remain current.

▪ Supervisors: shall ensure employees are trained before working with hazardous chemicals and that containers on site are labeled.

▪ Employees: shall follow safe-handling procedures, use required PPE, and report unlabeled containers or missing SDSs.

5.0 Program Requirements

Chemical Inventory & SDS

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall maintain a written inventory of hazardous chemicals present on its worksites and a current SDS for each.

▪ SDSs shall be readily accessible to employees on every shift and at each jobsite without barrier.

Labels & Warnings

▪ All containers of hazardous chemicals shall be labeled with the product identifier and hazard warnings; labels shall not be removed or defaced.

▪ Secondary and workplace containers shall be labeled per 29 CFR 1910.1200(f).

Multi-Employer Worksites

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall exchange SDS and hazard information with other on-site contractors and the general contractor on multi-employer worksites.

▪ Employees shall be informed of the hazards of non-routine tasks before performing them.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained at initial assignment and whenever a new chemical hazard is introduced to their work area.

▪ Training shall cover the program, the standard, hazard detection, physical and health hazards, protective measures, and reading labels and SDSs.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ The written program, chemical inventory, and SDS library shall be retained and available to employees and regulators.

▪ Training records shall be maintained per company policy and host-client requirements.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1926.59 / 29 CFR 1910.1200 — Hazard Communication

▪ Globally Harmonized System (GHS) — labeling and SDS format
