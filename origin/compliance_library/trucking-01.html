1.0 Purpose

This Driver Qualification Program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to ensure that every driver operating a commercial motor vehicle (CMV) is qualified, medically certified, and properly licensed. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR), 49 CFR Part 391.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} drivers who operate a commercial motor vehicle as defined in 49 CFR 390.5, including company drivers and owner-operators under our operating authority. All drivers shall meet these qualification requirements before operating a CMV.

3.0 Definitions

Commercial Motor Vehicle (CMV) — a vehicle used in commerce meeting the weight, passenger, or hazardous-materials thresholds of 49 CFR 390.5.

Driver Qualification (DQ) File — the file each motor carrier must maintain for every driver under 49 CFR 391.51.

CDL — Commercial Driver's License issued under 49 CFR Part 383.

Medical Examiner's Certificate — the certificate issued by a certified medical examiner on the National Registry per 49 CFR 391.43.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Safety Manager / Program Administrator: shall maintain the DQ file for each driver, verify licensing and medical certification, and ensure records remain current per Part 391.

▪ Supervisors / Dispatchers: shall not dispatch a driver whose qualification, medical certification, or license has lapsed.

▪ Drivers: shall hold a valid license and medical certificate, disclose all required information, and immediately report any license suspension, revocation, or disqualifying event.

5.0 Program Requirements

Driver Qualification File

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall maintain a Driver Qualification file for each driver in accordance with 49 CFR 391.51.

▪ The DQ file shall include the employment application (391.21), the motor vehicle record (MVR) inquiry and annual review (391.23, 391.25), the road test or equivalent (391.31/391.33), the medical examiner's certificate (391.43), and the annual list of violations (391.27).

▪ Records shall be retained for the periods specified in Part 391 and made available to auditors and host operators upon request.

Licensing & Medical Certification

▪ Each driver shall hold a valid CDL of the proper class and endorsements for the vehicle and cargo operated, per 49 CFR Part 383.

▪ Each driver shall be medically certified by an examiner listed on the FMCSA National Registry and shall carry a current medical examiner's certificate, per 49 CFR 391.41–391.43.

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall verify CDL status and medical certification prior to dispatch and shall not permit an unqualified driver to operate.

Background & MVR Review

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall investigate the driver's employment history for the preceding three years, including prior drug and alcohol testing history, per 49 CFR 391.23.

▪ Motor vehicle records shall be obtained at hire and reviewed at least annually to confirm continued qualification.

6.0 Training

▪ Drivers shall be oriented to {{COMPANY NAME}} policies, host-operator requirements, and their qualification obligations before first dispatch.

▪ Entry-level drivers shall receive training as required by 49 CFR Part 380 where applicable.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Driver Qualification files shall be maintained for the duration of employment and for three years thereafter, per 49 CFR 391.51.

▪ MVRs, annual reviews, and violation lists shall be retained and made available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 49 CFR Part 391 — Qualification of Drivers

▪ 49 CFR Part 383 — Commercial Driver's License Standards

▪ 49 CFR 391.41–391.49 — Physical Qualifications and Examinations
