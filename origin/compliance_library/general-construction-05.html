1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees who use ladders and stairways on construction sites. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees who use portable and fixed ladders and stairways during construction activities.

3.0 Definitions

Portable Ladder — a ladder that can be readily moved or carried, including step, extension, and straight ladders.

Competent Person — one capable of identifying ladder hazards and authorized to take prompt corrective measures.

Maximum Intended Load — the total weight of all employees, equipment, tools, and materials to be placed on the ladder.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Competent Person: shall inspect ladders periodically, oversee corrective action, and provide training.

▪ Supervisors: shall ensure ladders are appropriate for the task and in safe condition before use.

▪ Employees: shall inspect ladders before use, use three points of contact, and remove defective ladders from service.

5.0 Program Requirements

Selection & Capacity

▪ Ladders shall be capable of supporting their maximum intended load and shall be used only for the purpose for which they are designed, per 29 CFR 1926.1053.

▪ The proper ladder type and duty rating shall be selected for the task; metal ladders shall not be used near energized electrical equipment.

Setup & Use

▪ Non-self-supporting ladders shall be positioned at a 4:1 pitch (one foot out for every four feet of height).

▪ Ladders used to access an upper landing shall extend at least 3 feet above the landing or be secured with a grasping device.

▪ Employees shall face the ladder, maintain three points of contact, and shall not carry loads that could cause loss of balance.

Inspection & Maintenance

▪ Ladders shall be inspected by a competent person for visible defects periodically and after any occurrence that could affect safe use, per 29 CFR 1926.1053(b)(15).

▪ Defective ladders shall be tagged 'Do Not Use' and removed from service until repaired or discarded.

6.0 Training

▪ A competent person shall train each employee to recognize ladder hazards and use ladders properly, per 29 CFR 1926.1060.

▪ Retraining shall be provided when necessary to maintain understanding.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Training certifications and ladder inspection records shall be maintained and available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X — Stairways and Ladders (1926.1050–1060)
