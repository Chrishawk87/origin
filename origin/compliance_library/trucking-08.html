1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} for assessing hazards and selecting, providing, and using personal protective equipment (PPE) to protect drivers and shop personnel from injury. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1910 Subpart I.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees performing tasks where hazards requiring PPE are present, including cargo securement, coupling/uncoupling, load handling, fueling, and shop maintenance, as well as host-operator PPE requirements at delivery locations.

3.0 Definitions

PPE — equipment worn to minimize exposure to hazards, including eye, face, head, foot, hand, and high-visibility protection.

Hazard Assessment — a documented evaluation of a task or area to identify hazards requiring PPE, per 29 CFR 1910.132(d).

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall conduct and certify hazard assessments and ensure appropriate PPE is provided.

▪ Supervisors: shall enforce PPE use and ensure equipment is maintained and replaced.

▪ Employees: shall wear required PPE, inspect it before use, and report damaged or defective equipment.

5.0 Program Requirements

Hazard Assessment & Selection

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall conduct and certify a written hazard assessment to determine required PPE, per 29 CFR 1910.132(d).

▪ PPE shall be selected to protect against the identified hazards and shall meet the applicable ANSI/consensus standards.

Required PPE for Motor-Carrier Operations

▪ High-visibility apparel shall be worn where required by the host operator or when exposed to traffic or equipment movement.

▪ Foot, hand, eye, and head protection shall be worn as required for cargo securement, load handling, fueling, and shop work, per 29 CFR 1910.133/.135/.136/.138.

Maintenance & Inspection

▪ PPE shall be maintained in a sanitary and reliable condition; defective or damaged PPE shall be removed from service.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained on when PPE is necessary, what PPE to use, and how to don, doff, adjust, and maintain it, per 29 CFR 1910.132(f).

▪ Retraining shall occur when workplace or PPE changes render prior training obsolete.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ The written hazard-assessment certification and PPE training records shall be maintained and available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1910 Subpart I — Personal Protective Equipment

▪ 29 CFR 1910.132 — General Requirements and Hazard Assessment
