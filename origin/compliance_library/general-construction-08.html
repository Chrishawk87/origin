1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} for assessing hazards and selecting, providing, and using personal protective equipment (PPE) on construction worksites. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1926 Subpart E.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees and operations where construction hazards requiring PPE are present, including head, eye, face, hand, foot, hearing, and high-visibility protection.

3.0 Definitions

PPE — equipment worn to minimize exposure to hazards, including head, eye, face, hand, foot, and hearing protection.

Hazard Assessment — an evaluation of a task or work area to identify hazards requiring PPE.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Program Administrator: shall assess hazards and ensure appropriate PPE is provided and maintained.

▪ Supervisors: shall enforce PPE use and ensure equipment is inspected and replaced.

▪ Employees: shall wear required PPE, inspect it before use, and report damaged or defective equipment.

5.0 Program Requirements

Hazard Assessment & Selection

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall assess worksite hazards and provide appropriate PPE for the exposures identified, per 29 CFR 1926.95–.102.

▪ PPE shall meet the applicable ANSI/consensus standards for the type of protection provided.

Required PPE

▪ Head protection (hard hats) shall be worn where there is a possible danger of head injury, per 29 CFR 1926.100.

▪ Eye and face, hearing, hand, and foot protection shall be provided and worn as required by the identified hazards, per 29 CFR 1926.101–.102.

▪ High-visibility apparel shall be worn where employees are exposed to traffic or equipment movement.

Maintenance & Inspection

▪ PPE shall be maintained in a sanitary and reliable condition; defective or damaged PPE shall be removed from service.

6.0 Training

▪ Employees shall be trained on when PPE is necessary, what PPE to use, and how to don, doff, adjust, and maintain it.

▪ Retraining shall occur when workplace or PPE changes render prior training obsolete.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Hazard-assessment documentation and PPE training records shall be maintained and available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart E — Personal Protective and Life Saving Equipment

▪ 29 CFR 1926.100–.102 — Head, Hearing, Eye and Face Protection
