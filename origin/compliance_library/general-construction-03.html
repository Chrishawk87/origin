1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees who erect, use, move, alter, or dismantle scaffolds. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees who work on or around supported, suspended, or mobile scaffolds on construction worksites.

3.0 Definitions

Scaffold — any temporary elevated platform and its supporting structure used for supporting employees, materials, or both.

Qualified Person — one who, by degree, certificate, training, or experience, has demonstrated the ability to solve problems relating to the subject matter.

Competent Person — one capable of identifying scaffold hazards and authorized to take prompt corrective measures, per 29 CFR 1926.450.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Qualified Person: shall design and load-rate scaffolds so each supports its own weight plus at least 4 times the maximum intended load.

▪ Competent Person: shall supervise erection/alteration/dismantling, inspect scaffolds before each shift, and determine feasibility of fall protection during erection.

▪ Employees: shall use scaffolds only as trained, not overload them, and report defects.

5.0 Program Requirements

Capacity & Construction

▪ Each scaffold and component shall support, without failure, its own weight and at least 4 times the maximum intended load, per 29 CFR 1926.451(a).

▪ Platforms shall be fully planked, and scaffolds shall be erected on adequate, level foundations with proper bracing.

Fall & Falling-Object Protection

▪ Each employee on a scaffold more than 10 feet above a lower level shall be protected from falls by guardrails or a personal fall arrest system, per 29 CFR 1926.451(g).

▪ Employees shall be protected from falling objects by toeboards, screens, guardrail systems, or hard hats as required.

Access & Inspection

▪ Safe access shall be provided when scaffold platforms are more than 2 feet above or below a point of access; cross-braces shall not be used as a means of access.

▪ A competent person shall inspect the scaffold and components for visible defects before each work shift and after any occurrence that could affect structural integrity, per 29 CFR 1926.451(f)(3).

6.0 Training

▪ Each employee who works on a scaffold shall be trained by a qualified person on the hazards and procedures, per 29 CFR 1926.454.

▪ Employees who erect, alter, or dismantle scaffolds shall be trained by a competent person; retraining shall be provided when conditions change.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Training certifications and scaffold inspection records shall be maintained and available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L — Scaffolds (1926.450–454)
