1.0 Purpose

This Fall Protection Program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to protect employees from fall hazards on construction worksites. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M and shall provide fall protection for employees exposed to falls of 6 feet or more to a lower level.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} employees and subcontractors engaged in construction activities where a fall hazard of 6 feet or more exists, including work on leading edges, roofs, scaffolds, steel erection, excavations, and unprotected sides and edges.

3.0 Definitions

Guardrail System — a barrier erected to prevent employees from falling to lower levels, per 29 CFR 1926.502(b).

Personal Fall Arrest System (PFAS) — a system used to arrest an employee in a fall, consisting of an anchorage, connectors, and a full-body harness.

Controlled Access Zone — an area where certain work may take place without guardrails and access is controlled, per 29 CFR 1926.502(g).

Competent Person — one who is capable of identifying hazards and who has authorization to take prompt corrective measures.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Competent Person: shall identify fall hazards, select appropriate fall protection, inspect systems, and supervise their use.

▪ Supervisors: shall ensure fall protection is in place before employees are exposed and that all systems are used correctly.

▪ Employees: shall use provided fall protection, inspect their equipment before each use, and report defects or hazards.

5.0 Program Requirements

Duty to Have Fall Protection

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall provide fall protection for employees exposed to falls of 6 feet or more to a lower level, per 29 CFR 1926.501.

▪ Protection shall be provided by guardrail systems, safety net systems, or personal fall arrest systems as appropriate to the work.

▪ Holes and openings shall be protected by covers, guardrails, or fall arrest systems.

Systems Criteria & Use

▪ Fall protection systems shall meet the criteria of 29 CFR 1926.502.

▪ Personal fall arrest systems shall be rigged so that an employee can neither free-fall more than 6 feet nor contact a lower level, and anchorages shall support the required loads.

▪ Defective components shall be removed from service immediately.

Inspection

▪ A competent person shall inspect fall protection systems and equipment before use and after any event that could affect their integrity.

▪ Damaged or deteriorated equipment shall be removed from service and tagged.

6.0 Training

▪ Each employee exposed to fall hazards shall be trained by a competent person to recognize the hazards and the procedures to minimize them, per 29 CFR 1926.503.

▪ Retraining shall be provided when there are changes in the workplace, equipment, or when an employee shows inadequate understanding.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Written certification of training (employee name, date, trainer signature) shall be maintained per 29 CFR 1926.503(b).

▪ Inspection and equipment records shall be retained and available upon request.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M — Fall Protection (1926.500–503)
