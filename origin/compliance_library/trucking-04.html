1.0 Purpose

This program establishes the requirements of {{COMPANY NAME}} to maintain a drug- and alcohol-free commercial driving operation and to conduct the testing required of motor carriers. {{COMPANY NAME}} shall comply with 49 CFR Part 382 and the DOT testing procedures of 49 CFR Part 40.

2.0 Scope & Application

This program applies to all {{COMPANY NAME}} drivers who operate a commercial motor vehicle requiring a CDL and are therefore subject to DOT controlled-substances and alcohol testing. All covered drivers shall participate as a condition of employment.

3.0 Definitions

Safety-Sensitive Function — any on-duty function performed by a CDL driver as defined in 49 CFR 382.107.

Clearinghouse — the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse required for query and reporting under 49 CFR Part 382 Subpart G.

MRO — Medical Review Officer who reviews and verifies test results under 49 CFR Part 40.

4.0 Roles & Responsibilities

▪ Designated Employer Representative (DER): shall administer the testing program, receive results, remove drivers from safety-sensitive duty when required, and manage Clearinghouse queries and reporting.

▪ Supervisors: shall be trained in reasonable-suspicion determination and shall remove any driver reasonably suspected of prohibited use from duty.

▪ Drivers: shall submit to all required testing and shall not perform safety-sensitive functions while using prohibited substances or in violation of the alcohol rules.

5.0 Program Requirements

Required Testing

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall conduct pre-employment, random, reasonable-suspicion, post-accident, return-to-duty, and follow-up testing in accordance with 49 CFR Part 382.

▪ All specimen collection, testing, and result review shall follow the DOT procedures of 49 CFR Part 40.

▪ Random testing shall be conducted at or above the FMCSA minimum annual rates using a scientifically valid selection method.

Prohibitions & Removal

▪ A driver who tests positive, refuses a test, or otherwise violates Subpart B shall be immediately removed from safety-sensitive functions.

▪ A removed driver shall not return to duty until completing the return-to-duty process under 49 CFR Part 40 Subpart O.

Clearinghouse

▪ {{COMPANY NAME}} shall conduct pre-employment and annual queries of the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse and shall report required violations, per 49 CFR Part 382 Subpart G.

6.0 Training

▪ Drivers shall receive educational materials describing the program, prohibitions, and consequences, per 49 CFR 382.601.

▪ Supervisors shall receive at least 60 minutes of training on alcohol misuse and 60 minutes on controlled-substances use for reasonable-suspicion determinations, per 49 CFR 382.603.

7.0 Recordkeeping

▪ Testing records shall be retained for the periods specified in 49 CFR 382.401 and kept confidential and secure.

▪ Clearinghouse query and reporting records shall be maintained per Subpart G.

8.0 Regulatory References

▪ 49 CFR Part 382 — Controlled Substances and Alcohol Use and Testing

▪ 49 CFR Part 40 — Procedures for Transportation Workplace Drug and Alcohol Testing
